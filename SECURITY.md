# Security policy

Publishify stores people's unpublished writing, so we take security reports seriously.

## Reporting a vulnerability

**Please do not open a public issue.** Instead, use GitHub's private reporting:
**[Report a vulnerability](https://github.com/publishify/studio/security/advisories/new)**.

Please include:
- what you found and where (URL, file or endpoint),
- steps to reproduce,
- the impact you think it has.

We aim to acknowledge reports within **3 working days** and to keep you updated until the issue is fixed. With your permission, we will credit you in the release notes.

## Scope

In scope: this repository and the hosted service at `publishify.app`, including Studio (`/studio/`), the API (`/api/*`) and published pages (`/k/*`).

Out of scope: denial-of-service through volume, social engineering, reports from automated scanners without a working proof of concept, and issues in third-party services (Google Drive, Cloudflare) themselves.

## Please don't

- access, change or delete other people's data. Use your own test accounts.
- run tests that degrade the service for writers.

Thank you for helping keep writers' work safe.
